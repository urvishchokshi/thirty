Why I'm building Thirty

U

Urvish, founder of Thirty

Building Thirty · the Monday number for your salary

Apparently, my salary lasts until the 18th.

Not because of one ridiculous purchase. I found out by typing six numbers into a 60-second demo for the app I'm building.

Take someone earning Rs 75,000 a month. Before they really start spending, Rs 45,750 may already be spoken for: rent, bills, EMIs, card dues and money sent home.

That's 61% of their salary gone before dinners, cabs, weekends or the wedding they're travelling for next month.

The useful question isn't "What's my monthly budget?" It's "Given everything I already have to pay for, how much can I actually spend this week?"

That's what we're building with Thirty.
